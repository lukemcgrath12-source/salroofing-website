Colorbond & metal roofing

BlueScope-backed Colorbond installation rated for coastal Sydney conditions. Whether it's a new build or a full re-roof, we install to spec and register the manufacturer warranty.

  • New builds & full re-roofs
  • Coastal-grade materials
  • BlueScope warranty registration
02

Roof repairs & leak detection

We trace the source, not just patch the symptom. Cracked tiles, failed flashing, rusted sheets, leaks that have baffled other roofers — we find it and fix it properly.

  • Leak tracing & permanent fixes
  • Flashing, ridge capping, valleys
  • Workmanship stood behind
03

Roof replacement

When repairs no longer stack up, we handle the full replacement — tear-off, disposal, new install and clean-up. Straight pricing, no surprises, and we leave the site spotless.

  • Complete tear-off & disposal
  • Tile-to-metal conversions
  • Fixed, transparent quotes
04

Guttering & downpipes

New gutters, replacements, and downpipe work that actually moves water where it should. Done neat, done once. We'll also sort gutter guard if you're sick of clearing leaves.

  • New & replacement guttering
  • Downpipes & drainage
  • Gutter guard installation

Roof restoration

Bring a tired roof back to life — clean, repair, re-bed, re-point and re-coat. A fraction of the cost of replacement when the bones are still good.

  • High-pressure clean
  • Re-bedding & re-pointing
  • Protective re-coating
06

Roof inspections & reports

Buying, selling, or just want peace of mind? We'll give your roof an honest once-over and a written report — what's good, what needs doing, and what it'll cost. No scare tactics.

  • Written condition reports
  • Insurance documentation
